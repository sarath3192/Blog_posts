Should We Stop Developing AI?

Let's start at the beginning. When human civilization began, people discovered fire. At first, they didn't know how to control it or what risks it involved. Did they stop using it, or did they find ways to use it? They chose to use it despite the risks and found ways to control it for their benefit.

The point I want to make is that there is no need to fear AI because it might destroy us. If humans had feared that fire would destroy them, we might not be here today. We need to stop fearing AI and start thinking about how we can control it and use it for a purpose.

What if AI takes our jobs?

I believe the job market will shift. So far, we haven't seen AI independently produce a scientific breakthrough like Einstein's E = mc2, Darwin's theory, or the discovery of a new element. Until now, human thinking has been focused on Earth and the resources available here. With AI, our focus can shift to the resources of the universe and the finer details of nature, helping us find ways to reach them and use them to improve human life.

I am sure AI will shift people toward research jobs in different fields and help them make discoveries in areas we have never thought of, perhaps in a way similar to the software boom. People may have more time to do research and observe nature in detail instead of spending all their time writing code.

From a research point of view, AI and humans can work together. They complement each other as they explore the vast universe and discover new things.
